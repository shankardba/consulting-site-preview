Data architectures

Every business has data. The architecture decides what you can do with it.

There are four common architectures: rows in a spreadsheet, related tables, flexible documents and connected graphs. Each is brilliant at some jobs and painful at others. Watch them work on the same small business.

The four architectures

Spreadsheets

Rows and columns that one person can own. Unbeatable for quick math, but fragile once people share them.

Relational

Tables linked by IDs, with rules at the door. The safe home for customers, orders and money.

Document

The real answer is usually “more than one”

A growing business ends up with a spreadsheet for planning, a database for orders, documents for its website content and sometimes a graph for referrals or recalls. The hard part isn’t picking one architecture. It’s making them work together so everyone sees the same truth. That’s data integration, and it’s what we do.
