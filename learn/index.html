Every business has data. Its shape decides the tool.

There are four common shapes: rows in a spreadsheet, related tables, flexible documents and connected graphs. Each is brilliant at some jobs and painful at others. Watch them work on the same small bakery, then test yourself.

The four shapes

Spreadsheets

Rows and columns that one person can own. Unbeatable for quick math, but fragile once people share them.

Relational

Which shape fits?

Pick the best shape for each kind of data. There’s often more than one workable answer; this is the one that fits most naturally.

The real answer is usually “more than one”

A growing business ends up with a spreadsheet for planning, a database for orders, documents for its website content and sometimes a graph for referrals or recalls. The hard part isn’t picking one shape. It’s making them work together so everyone sees the same truth. That’s data integration, and it’s what I do.
