The short version

✓ Great for

  • Lists one person owns: budgets, price lists, quick plans
  • Fast math, what-if scenarios and charts
  • Sharing a snapshot with someone who just needs to read it
  • Prototyping, before you know what you need

✗ Watch out for

  • The same customer or product typed over and over
  • Several people editing at once, with formulas getting overwritten
  • No rules: “twelve” in a number column silently breaks totals
  • Gets slow and fragile past tens of thousands of rows

Perfect for one person’s lists and quick math. When several people share it, or it holds your customers, it’s time for a database.

Common tools

Excel, Google Sheets, Airtable (part spreadsheet, part database)
